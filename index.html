The button saves the file. It does not open by itself. That is normal.

  1. Tap the button. Wait until it says the download finished.
  2. Tap the finished download. On a tablet that is the bar at the top, or the Downloads app.
  3. Tap Install. Install it over the Jack's Desk already on the tablet. Do not uninstall first.
  4. Open Jack's Desk.

If the tablet asks, allow installs from the browser.
